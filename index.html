FAQ

Frequently asked.

What tools do you work with?

R, SPSS, Minitab, Excel, and Power BI are my core stack. I pick the tool that fits the question, not the other way around.

How fast is your turnaround?

Most projects land in 3 to 7 days depending on data size and scope. Cleaning-heavy work takes longer; one-off analyses often come back in 48 hours.

Do you work with confidential data?

Yes. I follow standard data handling protocols and can sign NDAs before any data exchange. Your data stays yours.

Can you present findings to my team?

Absolutely. Every project includes a written report; live walkthroughs can be arranged on request.

What does a typical engagement look like?

Brief, data audit, cleaning, analysis, visualization, report. You see progress at every stage, not just the final deck.
